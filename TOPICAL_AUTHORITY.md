# Topical Authority — pintu.kacafilm.co.id

## Role and boundary

This domain should become a focused reference for selecting, specifying, installing, inspecting, and maintaining film on glazed doors in homes, offices, shops, hospitality spaces, and public buildings. It helps owners, designers, facility teams, installers, and buyers separate privacy, manifestation, solar, decorative, fragment-retention, and security goals before following the existing redirect to the parent commercial site. Film must never be described as automatically converting ordinary glass into compliant safety glazing, fire-rated glazing, a structural barrier, or a tested forced-entry system. Glass/film compatibility, thermal stress, impact/fall protection, egress, powered-door sensors, fire doors, worksite safety, electrical interfaces, and performance claims require project-specific evidence, current authoritative requirements, manufacturer approval, tested assemblies, and competent review.

## Context used

- `/` redirects permanently to `https://kacafilm.co.id`; the parent domain therefore owns live commercial browsing and purchase intent.

## Internal-link rule

Every article links to its parent hub. PKF-01 decision pages lead to identification (PKF-02), safety (PKF-03), performance (PKF-04 through PKF-07), compatibility (PKF-08), and survey (PKF-09). PKF-14 application pages link to hardware/sensor coordination (PKF-11), workmanship (PKF-10), diagnosis (PKF-12), maintenance (PKF-13), and evidence review (PKF-16). Commercial links point to the existing parent-domain redirect only after the reader has a qualified brief. Safety, fire, egress, impact, powered-door, and security pages cross-link to their explicit evidence gates; no location page is created.
