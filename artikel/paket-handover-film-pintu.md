### KR-04

- **Original sources:** [SNI 9144-1:2022 laminated safety glass catalog](https://pesta.bsn.go.id/produk/detail/14416-sni9144-12022), [SNI 9144-2:2022 tempered safety glass catalog](https://pesta.bsn.go.id/produk/detail/14417-sni9144-22022), and [ISO 29584:2015 pendulum impact abstract](https://www.iso.org/standard/62469.html).
- **Purpose for this article:** Separate safety-glass material identity, impact classification, intended location, and applied-film claims.
- **Safe grounded facts:** A safety-glass standard title is not evidence that existing door glass conforms. A test classification describes the tested specimen and method. “Safety glass,” “safety film,” fragment retention, and code-appropriate glazing are not interchangeable labels.
- **Limits:** Public abstracts do not supply clauses or project thresholds. Do not declare an existing door safe, allow continued use after breakage, or claim retrofit film converts unknown glass into compliant safety glazing. Apply GATE-01, GATE-02, and GATE-04.

### KR-09

- **Original sources:** [IWFA safety and security guidance](https://iwfa.com/safety-and-security/), [ISO 16936-1:2020 forced-entry abstract](https://www.iso.org/standard/75209.html), [ISO 16936-3:2005 manual-attack abstract](https://www.iso.org/standard/38171.html), and [3M safety/security installation bulletin](https://multimedia.3m.com/mws/media/1704892O/security-window-film.pdf).
- **Purpose for this article:** Control safety/security language and anchor it to a defined threat, test, and full glazing assembly.
- **Safe grounded facts:** ISO 16936 parts define particular forced-entry test methods rather than a universal “secure” label. IWFA says threat events need different protocols and installation techniques and states it is unaware of film with ballistic properties. 3M's bulletin says its named films are not bulletproof and not designed to stop intruders; listed mitigation applications require exact specifications and, in some cases, a named attachment system.
- **Limits:** Never promise burglar-proof, bulletproof, a generic delay time, structural capacity, or continued safe door operation after breakage. Marketing demonstrations are not test reports. Apply GATE-04 and GATE-05.

### KR-12

- **Original sources:** [3M safety/security installation bulletin, Revision C January 2025](https://multimedia.3m.com/mws/media/1704892O/security-window-film.pdf), [IWFA architectural installation overview](https://iwfa.com/installation-process/), and [GGF window-film document index](https://www.ggf.org.uk/downloads/window-film/).
- **Purpose for this article:** Support process literacy, site controls, and evidence-based acceptance without turning articles into hazardous DIY instructions.
- **Safe grounded facts:** 3M's named-product bulletin requires current technical documents, inspection of glass, written documentation of uncorrected defects, clean/dust-controlled conditions, and product-specific installation. It also acknowledges that some edge contamination or exterior optical limitations can be unavoidable in stated conditions. GGF lists industry documents on installation and visual quality, but full text access is gated.
- **Limits:** Do not transplant 3M dimensions or cure periods to another film, allow blade work on uncertain coated glass, or give powered-door/fire-door DIY instructions. Obtain current product instructions and acceptance criteria under GATE-08.
